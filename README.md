## About

12 years in the industry, turning fragile, tribal operational knowledge into platforms that are repeatable, observable and properly versioned.

I'm a father based in Graz, and a suspicious amount of my free time goes into a Kubernetes cluster humming away in the room next door. Whatever feels too risky to try at work, I test at home first. [hauptmann.dev](https://hauptmann.dev) is served from that cluster, and its [status page](https://status.hauptmann.dev/status/pmh-core) is public.
